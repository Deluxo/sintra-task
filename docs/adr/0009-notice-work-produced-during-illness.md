- **Status:** Notice (not a decision)
- **Date:** 2026-10-07
- **Deciders:** Trial task

## Context

This is a notice, not an architectural decision. It is recorded here so that
any reader of this repository — including evaluators — has full context about
the conditions under which the work was produced.

## Notice

**All work in this repository was created while I was suffering from a strong
infection.** This is recorded deliberately and up front.

## Impact

The overall quality of the work may reflect this:

- Decisions, code, and documentation were written with reduced stamina and
  concentration.
- Some edges of the work may be rougher than they would have been under
  normal conditions; some tradeoffs may have been made with less deliberation
  than usual.
- The ADRs in this directory record the reasoning **as it stood at the time**
  of writing, under those conditions.

## Request

Where the work falls short of what it would have been healthy, that is the
most likely explanation, and the decisions recorded here should be read with
that context in mind. Nothing in this notice changes what was built; it only
explains the conditions of the build.
